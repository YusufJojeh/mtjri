# Design System

## Overview

MTJRii / StoreGo presents a dark cinematic SaaS landing system wrapped around practical commerce surfaces. The public hero uses a deep navy canvas with localized blue and gold glows, glass cards, and product screenshots inside device-like frames. The product UI screenshots switch to clean white admin surfaces with black typography and green StoreGo accents. The visual tone is operator-focused: premium, organized, and built around real dashboard, theme, order, payment, and catalog screens.

## Colors

- **Deep Navy**: `#081223` - primary cinematic landing surface.
- **Panel Navy**: `#050B16` - framed media cards and player chrome.
- **Section Navy**: `#0B1630` - secondary dark background.
- **Brand Blue**: `#1578D8` - motion glow, active data accents, and CTA energy.
- **Action Blue**: `#0D8BFF` - bright call-to-action and active UI marks.
- **Brand Gold**: `#FFC107` - badges, highlights, and end-card emphasis.
- **StoreGo Green**: `#10B981` - commerce/admin success accent from dashboard UI.
- **White Text**: `#FFFFFF` - primary text on dark surfaces.
- **Soft Text**: `#D8E4F8` - secondary copy on dark surfaces.
- **Admin Surface**: `#F8FAFC` - clean dashboard and storefront screenshot ground.

## Typography

- **Primary Sans**: Instrument Sans variable. Used for all promo titles, labels, stats, and body text.
- **Hero Scale**: 84-116px, heavy weight, tight but readable line-height.
- **Section Headings**: 54-72px, semibold to bold.
- **Labels**: 18-22px, uppercase, strong tracking, blue or gold accents.
- **Body Text**: 28-36px for promo readability; short copy only.

## Elevation

Depth comes from layered glass cards, thin white borders at 10-14% opacity, large dark shadows, and localized radial glows instead of flat fills. Screenshots are treated as physical product surfaces: rounded frames, subtle border highlights, and slow camera drift. The dark landing layer should feel cinematic, while the product screenshots should remain crisp and inspectable.

## Components

- **Cinematic Hero Frame**: dark navy background, live product frame, logo badge, layered screenshots, and blue/gold glows.
- **Commerce Dashboard Cards**: white admin UI with stat panels, recent orders, top products, and QR code.
- **Theme Gallery Grid**: storefront theme previews in a large admin grid with active selection state.
- **Glass Proof Cards**: dark translucent cards with thin borders and compact commerce claims.
- **Motion Player Frame**: framed Remotion presentation surface with header chrome and badge.
- **CTA End Card**: centered logo, large platform promise, and gold/blue action treatment.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use `#081223`, `#050B16`, `#1578D8`, and `#FFC107` as the main promo palette.
- Keep real product screenshots large enough to read at 1920x1080.
- Frame screenshots as premium surfaces with borders, shadows, and slow camera movement.
- Use blue and gold highlights to connect the cinematic landing to the Remotion palette.
- Keep copy short and operational: stores, themes, checkout, products, orders, analytics.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the promo into a generic SaaS landing page.
- Do not use a one-note blue-only palette; gold and green must appear as active accents.
- Do not bury product screenshots behind blur or dark overlays.
- Do not use stock imagery where real platform screens exist.
- Do not use beige, purple gradients, or unrelated marketing decoration.
